## 💡 Featured Projects

### 🐄 [Pashu Pehchan](https://github.com/RoshanGowdaR) — Cattle & Buffalo Breed Recognition
AI-based deep learning framework for visual breed recognition of indigenous Indian cattle and buffaloes.
- Built with **MobileNetV2**, **Flask**, and **Gemini API** integration
- Trained on 10 breeds with **~92% validation accuracy**
- Multilingual chat-based frontend with breed ID certificate display

### 🎓 [Intelligent Placement Management System (IPMS)](https://github.com/RoshanGowdaR/Intelligent-Placement-Management-System)
Full-stack campus placement management platform (a.k.a. Pro Placement Plus), v3.5, with admin/company/student roles.
- Subject of a published research paper (IJSREM, May 2026)
- Being extended into a conference paper submission

### 🎙️ StudyBuddy — 🏆 1st Place, NeuroNex 2026
Full-stack **RAG application** with real-time voice interaction for personalized studying.

### 👁️ Smart Blind Stick — AI Voice Navigation System
Assistive hardware-software system using **YOLOv11** and **gTTS**, integrated with Raspberry Pi, Arduino, GPS, and GSM for real-time obstacle detection and voice guidance.

### 🌾 KrishiMitra
Multilingual AI agriculture chatbot built to help farmers access information in their own language.

### 🏥 Smart Health
AI-driven health centre and supply chain management system for Indian PHCs/CHCs, built with **Google/Firebase** services at the Hack2Skill "Build with AI" hackathon.

### 🏘️ Local Event & Festival Information Aggregator
VTU Open Elective Course project — a website that aggregates local event and festival information for communities.
